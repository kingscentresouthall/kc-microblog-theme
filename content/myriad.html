Myriad Pathway

Training for people called to lead a new Christian community.

A two-year pathway that helps lay leaders gather a team, grow in confidence and nurture a new worshipping community.

What Myriad offers

Training, mentoring and a community of fellow leaders.

Myriad is for people who feel called to start and lead new expressions of church. The pathway combines practical learning, theological reflection, mentoring and support as your community develops.

LengthTwo-year pathway
PatternUsually every six weeks in term time
SessionsSaturday mornings at The Kings Centre

The journey

From calling to sustainable leadership.

The programme supports both the leader and the community they are gathering.

Discern your calling

Explore the people, place and purpose at the heart of the new community.

Build a team

Learn how to invite others, share responsibility and lead collaboratively.

Help people grow in faith

Create accessible practices of worship, learning, service and discipleship.

Reflect and adapt

Use mentoring and peer support to learn from experience and keep developing.
