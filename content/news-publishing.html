Publishing news

A simple way for colleagues to share an update.

The private publishing tool helps authorised team members prepare a clear story, add approved media and send it to Kings Centre Southall News.

For authorised colleagues

Write once, then review or publish.

Use the publisher for ordinary news, project updates and event stories. You can create a review draft when someone else needs to check the wording, or publish directly when you have authority to do so.

AccessSign in with your approved account

Before you send

Keep every update clear, accurate and safe.

The form includes a final approval check. These are the points to confirm first.

Lead with the news

Use a short, specific headline and put the most important information in the first paragraph.

Check the details

Confirm names, dates, times, links and contact information before creating the post.

Record consent

Only use names, stories, photographs or recordings that are approved for public use.

Protect people

Never include safeguarding reports, confidential case information or private contact details.

✓

Safeguarding concerns need a different route.

Do not put a concern into a news draft. Use our safeguarding information to contact the right person.
