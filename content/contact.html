What you’ll find here

A welcoming community space in the heart of Southall Green.

The Kings Centre is based in a church and community building used for local activities, events and support. The wider centre hosts coffee mornings, wellbeing groups and social activities for older residents, with free Wi-Fi and refreshments available during many activities.
