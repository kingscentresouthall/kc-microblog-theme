Local projects

Practical support for ideas that serve local people.

We help eligible church-led projects in Southall and Hounslow turn good local ideas into useful, sustainable action.

Support that fits the project

Funding is one part of the conversation.

Projects may need a small grant, advice, connections or help thinking through delivery. We start by understanding the idea, the local need and who will benefit.
