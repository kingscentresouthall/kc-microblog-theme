For teams and organisations

Training for working well across cultures and faiths

Interactive learning for people working in diverse communities, tailored to the setting and the people in the room.

One day or tailored

Faith awareness

Learn how different faith traditions shape people’s lives and how to build respectful relationships. A typical day includes conversations with local faith leaders and visits to places of worship.

Half day or full day

Cultural awareness

Explore how culture affects our assumptions, communication and decisions—often without us noticing—and practise working more effectively across difference.

Built around your needs

Bespoke training

For schools, councils, charities, businesses, clergy and community groups. We begin with a conversation about your aims, audience, budget and setting.

Practical guidance

Advice and consultancy

Talk through a challenge involving faith, culture, community relationships or inclusive practice with an experienced member of our team.

Who is it for?Teams serving or working in diverse communities.
What does it cost?Quoted according to your needs and budget.
How long?Usually half a day or a full day.
Where?At the Centre, locally or by arrangement.

For church and community leaders

Support to turn a calling into practical local action.

Myriad equips lay leaders through training, mentoring and ongoing support.

Two-year pathway

Myriad leadership training

Training and mentoring for lay leaders who want to gather and lead a new Christian community. The programme covers leadership, building a team, helping people grow in faith, reflection and ongoing support.

Every six weeks in term time

What to expect

Saturday morning sessions at The Kings Centre Southall, usually from 9.30am to 1pm. The programme is currently free for new lay leaders and includes lunch.
