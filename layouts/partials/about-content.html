About The Kings Centre

Local knowledge. Skilled people. A belief that communities thrive together.

We are a Southall-based charity helping people and organisations learn across cultural, religious and generational differences—and turn that learning into practical local action.

Why we’re here

Difference should be a strength, not a barrier.

Southall is home to people from many cultures, faiths and backgrounds. That creates enormous energy and possibility, as well as questions that cannot be answered with a one-size-fits-all approach.

The Kings Centre creates space for people to listen, learn and work together. We support young people, equip local leaders and help organisations serve diverse communities with greater confidence.

Our roots

Made in Southall.

The Kings Centre was established in 2014 and is based at St John’s Church in Southall Green. Our work grows from long-term relationships across community organisations, faith groups, schools, public services and local residents.

We value professional expertise, but we also know that lived experience matters. Our training is shaped by the realities of one of London’s most diverse communities.
